Our Story

Why we started CityPlay

Finding a turf, a pickleball court, or a group of people to play with used to mean a dozen WhatsApp messages and a lot of guesswork. CityPlay started as a simple idea: make it effortless to find and book a game near you in Gandhinagar.

Today, we connect players with verified venues and local hosts, so anyone can turn a free evening into a game — solo, with friends, or with people they'll meet for the first time on the field.
